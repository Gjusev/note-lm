# Brag Plan: note-lm

## What is this app?

note-lm is a local-first research notebook where every saved claim stays attached to the exact source version and evidence that supports it.

## The angle

Research changes. Evidence should remember. This is a quiet, confident product film about the moment a document changes without silently rewriting the researcher's work.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

An evidence card reads `v1` beside a highlighted quote. The source updates to `v2`; the quote does not move. On-screen: **Research changes. Evidence remembers.**

## Key moments (the middle)

- A real note-lm library opens with a single click on **Beispiel laden**.
- A highlighted passage becomes an anchored claim, with the original document and its version visibly retained.
- A re-import produces a review proposal and an evidence matrix, not a silent rewrite.

## Outro / punchline

The matrix resolves into the mark and the line: **Every claim opens its evidence.**

## User flow worth showing

Open a notebook -> save a claim from a quoted source passage -> re-import a changed source and review the evidence linked to the original version.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet editorial research film, precise rather than loud
- Interpretation: restrained motion, generous negative space, tactile interface cues, and a warm paper palette. The film earns attention through a specific research moment rather than generic AI claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#f6f4f0`
- Accent: `#a44b3b`
- Text: `#292720`
- Display font: Segoe UI Variable / Aptos / Helvetica Neue fallback stack
- Body font: Segoe UI Variable / Aptos / Helvetica Neue fallback stack
- Strongest visual element: the three-column research workspace with a source navigator, working document, and evidence inspector.

## Share copy (draft)

note-lm is a local-first research notebook where every claim opens the evidence and source version behind it. Research changes; your evidence trail does not disappear.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm, restrained professional bed with tactile interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, fade in under the hook and fade down beneath the final mark
- Music treatment: soft opening, then enough presence for the workspace reveals; no vocal layer
- Music cue guidance: bundled 120.19 BPM cue preset; major matrix/mark reveal locks near 17.02s and 20.02s. Sequential source/claim/review cues use 7.02s, 7.52s, and 8.02s, then hold long enough to read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the paper field and accent marker gain a barely perceptible presence from RMS. No waveform or equalizer imagery.
- SFX posture: sparse; a low-risk click for the import action and a warm soft-impact for the final mark
- Audio-coupled moments: click on **Beispiel laden**; three evidence states arrive sequentially; mark lands with the final impact.
- Restraint rule: audio supports the documentary-like interface motion and never turns the research workflow into a game.
