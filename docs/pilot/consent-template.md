# E3 pilot — informed consent template

> Fill-in template. Print or hand over electronically before the session starts.
> Hand the participant the version in their strongest session language if a
> translation exists; the English master governs. **This is a plain-language
> research participation agreement written for a small local usability study. It
> is not legal advice and does not claim or certify GDPR compliance** — the
> wording is intended to be consistent with GDPR principles (purpose limitation,
> data minimization, withdrawal), and any formal assessment is out of scope of
> this template.

> Bracketed `[...]` fields are filled per session. Nothing else is editable

## Participation agreement — usability session for the note-lm research notebook

Study: **E3 pilot usability session**, conducted by `[moderator name, role]`
on `[date]`, on `[participant's laptop / provided laptop]`.

### 1. What happens in the session

You will use the note-lm desktop application on a Windows computer while
thinking aloud: importing a document, asking it questions, saving statements,
and working with a changed document version. The session takes about 60–90
minutes. You may stop at any time, skip any task, and take breaks; you do not
need to give a reason.

We are testing the software, not you. There are no right answers, and nothing
you do can be "wrong".

### 2. What is processed, and where it stays

- All of your activity in the app (documents you import, questions you ask,
  statements you save) is stored **locally on the session computer only**, in
  the application's own data folder. The pilot does not upload it anywhere.
- The session recordings and observation notes described in §3 stay **on the
  session computer** unless you tick a box below asking otherwise.
- If you install the app on **your own laptop**, that data stays on your
  laptop; you can delete it by uninstalling the app and removing its data
  folder (the moderator will show you where).
- The app itself can use remote AI services only if you explicitly configure
  one with your own key during the session. If you use your own remote API key,
  the requests that the app sends to that provider are covered by that
  provider's terms — we will point out where the app shows you which provider
  is used, and you are free to choose a local-only configuration instead.

### 3. What we record

Tick what you agree to (both optional; you can take part without either):

- [ ] **Screen recording** of the app window during the tasks. The recording
  stays on the session computer, is used only to re-check observations, and is
  deleted at your request or `[default: after the report is written / date]`.
- [ ] **Verbatim quotes** from your think-aloud in an internal analysis report
  and in the open-source project's public evaluation report, attributed only as
  "participant", never with your name. Quotes are shared with you on request
  before publication if you ask for that today.

The observer's notes (timestamps, errors, quotes) and the questionnaire are the
minimum record; if you decline both boxes above, the session runs on notes
only, with quotes held back until you review them.

### 4. Your data rights in this session

- **Withdrawal:** you can stop participating at any moment. On request we
  delete your session's recording, notes and questionnaire; if quotes were
  already handed to the analysis, we remove them there too. Withdrawing does
  not require a reason and has no consequences for you.
- **Access:** you can ask to see everything recorded about you.
- **Anonymity:** reports refer to you by a participant code only (e.g. `P3`).
  The key linking code to name, if collected at all, is kept on the session
  computer and not shared.

### 5. What we do with the results

Findings inform the open-source note-lm project. Published results are
descriptive (small sample, no statistical generalization) and identify no
individual. Nothing from your own documents or keys is included in any report;
if you import your own document, we quote only from the prepared sample
materials unless a quote from yours is essential and you approved it in §3.

### 6. Contact

Questions, access requests, or withdrawal later on:
`[contact name / email, reachable at least until the stated deletion date]`.
